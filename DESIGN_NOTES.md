# Equal Horizons — Bright Anime Horizon Direction

## Core idea
A cheerful, hand-authored cel-shaded world built around the idea of many paths meeting at one shared horizon. The visual centerpiece is not a generated background, stock illustration, or Spline template. It is an original animated SVG environment with separate motion systems for clouds, flowers, the floating garden, orbiting idea seeds, petals, breeze lines, and sunlight.

## Visual system
- Bright sunrise blues, warm yellow, fresh green, coral, and violet
- Heavy navy outlines and flat cel-shaded surfaces
- Oversized editorial typography paired with an italic serif accent
- Full-width sections and ruled editorial layouts instead of repetitive SaaS cards
- Purpose-built project diagrams rather than stock images
- Original horizon-window and logo artwork

## Motion system
- Pointer-responsive 3D tilt on the hero environment
- Independent cloud drift at different depths
- Flower and tree wind loops
- Floating island movement
- Orbiting idea seeds
- Drifting petals and animated breeze strokes
- Scroll-linked hero depth
- Fixed page progress line
- Sticky four-stage process scene with active-step transitions
- Scroll-drawn route and moving sunrise
- Project artwork entering from depth as each row reaches the viewport
- Reduced-motion fallback through `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Reference boundary
Los Altos Hacks was used only as a reference for confidence, motion density, and world-building. No artwork, text, models, or layout was copied.


## Removed template patterns
The public site intentionally excludes technology-stack showcases, emoji checklists, generic feature badges, fake social proof, and “what makes us different” card grids. Technical implementation details remain in the README only.
